Frequently asked questions

Live Online CPC Classes FAQ

Are these CPC classes really live?

Yes. The Boot Camp uses scheduled live online evening classes, so students can learn in real time and ask questions.

Can a beginner take the live CPC Boot Camp?

Yes. No prior coding experience is required. The program is designed to build coding knowledge from the ground up, although students should expect an intensive pace and independent work outside class.

How many hours of live CPC training are included?

There are 10 scheduled sessions from 5:00–10:00 PM Eastern, targeting approximately 45 hours of live instruction after scheduled breaks.

Are coding books included?

No. Students purchase current ICD-10-CM, CPT® Professional, and HCPCS Level II coding books.

Is the CPC certification exam included?

No. The CPC certification exam fee is separate from Boot Camp tuition.

Can I talk to the instructor before registering?

Yes. Call or text Jenifer at (574) 213-8793 with questions about the schedule, course format, required books, or whether the live program fits you.

Have questions about live CPC training?

Talk directly with Jenifer before you enroll. Ask about the live class schedule, the accelerated format, or whether the Boot Camp is the right CPC training path for you.
