The goal

Turn complicated risk adjustment concepts into practical documentation habits.

Providers should not be expected to become coders. Education should help them understand how clear, specific, clinically supported documentation reflects the care delivered and gives coding teams the information they need.

01

Improve documentation clarity

Help providers document chronic conditions, status, evaluation, and management in a clear and supportable way.

02

Strengthen collaboration

Bridge communication gaps among providers, coders, auditors, educators, and practice leadership.

03

Support audit readiness

Reinforce documentation integrity and reduce reliance on vague, copied-forward, or unsupported diagnoses.

Frequently asked questions

About provider documentation training

Is this a coding class for physicians?

No. The education is provider-friendly and focused on practical documentation habits, clinical support, chronic condition capture, and the connection between documentation and accurate coding.

Can a session be delivered virtually?

Yes. Virtual lunch-and-learns and workshops can be scheduled for practices and healthcare teams across the United States.

Can the presentation be customized?

Yes. Topics, examples, length, and audience level can be adapted to the organization’s priorities.

Who should coordinate the session?

Common contacts include coding managers, risk adjustment leaders, provider educators, CDI leaders, practice managers, population health teams, and revenue cycle leadership.

Interested in provider documentation education for your team?

Start with a conversation about your audience, training goals, preferred format, and the documentation challenges your organization is seeing.
