Our Services

Focused auditing support for your organization

HCC Coding Accuracy Audits

Review selected charts and coding decisions for accuracy, documentation supportability, and recurring error patterns. Receive actionable findings to inform quality improvement.

Second-Level Audit Reviews

Evaluate existing audit determinations, including whether cited coding errors and auditor conclusions are justified by the documentation and applicable coding guidance.

Provider Documentation Reviews

Assess whether chronic conditions and reported diagnoses are supported in the medical record and identify documentation concerns appropriate for provider education.

Flexible Engagements

Start with the support your team needs

Pilot Coding Quality Assessment

A defined sample of records reviewed against an agreed audit scope, followed by a clear findings summary and practical recommendations. A useful starting point before a larger engagement.

Ongoing or Overflow Audit Support

Periodic quality reviews, supplemental audit capacity, or independent second-level reviews for organizations with established coding and risk adjustment operations.

Engagement size, review methodology, turnaround, reporting format, and pricing are established by agreement. Work involving protected health information begins only after appropriate contractual, privacy, and secure-access arrangements are in place.

Audit Findings to Action

Auditing and education can work together

An audit can identify recurring patterns. Targeted education helps teams understand those patterns and improve future coding decisions.

Organizations may also request HCC risk adjustment team training and provider documentation education tailored to their needs.

Our approach

  • Agree on audit scope and standards
  • Review authorized records securely
  • Document supported findings and rationale
  • Share a clear summary of quality themes
  • Discuss optional follow-up education
