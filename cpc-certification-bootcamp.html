Who it's for

No prior coding experience is required. This program is designed for learners who want a structured, accelerated path into professional medical coding.

This may be a good fit if you are:

Frequently asked questions

CPC Boot Camp FAQ

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. The program is designed to build coding knowledge from the ground up. Students should, however, be prepared for an accelerated pace and independent study between live sessions.

How many hours of training are included?

There are 10 scheduled evening sessions from 5:00–10:00 PM Eastern. With scheduled breaks built in, the program targets approximately 45 hours of live instruction.

Are the coding books included?

No. Students purchase their own current ICD-10-CM, CPT® Professional, and HCPCS Level II coding books before class begins.

Is the CPC exam included?

No. The CPC certification exam fee is separate from boot camp tuition.

Is this the same as a CPC exam review?

No. This is an accelerated training program for students building coding knowledge. MediCode Bootcamp also plans a separate one-day CPC Certification Exam Review for students who have already completed coding education and are preparing to test.

Does completing the boot camp guarantee I will pass the CPC exam?

No. The boot camp provides education, guided practice, and exam preparation support, but no training program can guarantee a passing exam score.
