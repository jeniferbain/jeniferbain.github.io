Accelerated CPC training

Build your coding knowledge from the ground up

This is not a one-day exam review. The CPC Certification Boot Camp is an accelerated training program designed for students who want live instruction, guided coding practice, and a structured path through the core areas of professional medical coding.

Live instruction

Learn in real time with an instructor who explains concepts, demonstrates code-book navigation, and answers questions.

Hands-on coding

Practice applying coding guidelines, selecting codes, using modifiers, and working through realistic coding scenarios.

Accelerated format

Use focused live instruction plus off-nights and weekends for reading, workbook activities, and independent coding practice.

Required books

Students purchase their own coding books

Before the first class, students must have current editions of:

  • ICD-10-CM
  • CPT® Professional
  • HCPCS Level II

Coding books are not included in tuition.

Who it's for

Designed for aspiring medical coders

No prior coding experience is required. This program is designed for learners who want a structured, accelerated path into professional medical coding.

This may be a good fit if you are:

  • Completely new to medical coding
  • Exploring medical coding as a career change
  • Working in healthcare and ready to learn coding
  • Preparing to work toward the CPC credential
  • Someone who learns best with live instructor support

Expect an intensive pace

Students should plan to complete reading, workbook activities, and coding practice outside the live sessions. The goal is to combine focused live instruction with structured independent work.

Frequently asked questions

CPC Boot Camp FAQ

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. The program is designed to build coding knowledge from the ground up.

How many hours of training are included?

There are 10 scheduled evening sessions from 5:00–10:00 PM Eastern, targeting approximately 45 hours of live instruction after scheduled breaks.

Are coding books included?

No. Students purchase their own current ICD-10-CM, CPT® Professional, and HCPCS Level II coding books.

Is the CPC exam included?

No. The CPC certification exam fee is separate from boot camp tuition.

Is this the same as a CPC exam review?

No. This is an accelerated training program for students building coding knowledge. The CPC Certification Exam Review is a separate program for students who have already completed coding education and are preparing to test.
