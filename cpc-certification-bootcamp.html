Frequently asked questions

CPC Specialty Boot Camp FAQ

Is the former three-week CPC Boot Camp still offered?

No. We now offer shorter, topic-focused specialty boot camps. For comprehensive training, explore our self-study CPC course.

Can I take just one class?

Yes. Choose individual subject-focused sessions based on your needs.

Are these classes suitable for beginners?

Many topics welcome developing coders, but the recommended experience may vary by subject. Contact us if you are unsure where to begin.

Are the classes CEU-approved?

CEU availability depends on the specific class and any applicable approval. We will clearly identify approved CEUs on individual class announcements.

Are books or CPC exam attempts included?

Required materials and exam fees vary by offering. Specialty boot camps do not automatically include coding books or certification exam attempts.
