Why weekly instructor sessions matter

Self-paced learning gives you control over when you study. Weekly instructor sessions give you recurring opportunities to bring questions, reinforce difficult concepts, and stay connected to an instructor while you work through the program.

That makes this option especially useful for students who cannot commit to an intensive evening boot camp but do not want a completely independent course.
