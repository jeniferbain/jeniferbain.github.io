Beginner roadmap

What does it take to start a medical coding career?

There is no single path for every student, but most beginners need to move through a few important stages. The webinar will explain each one in more detail.

1

Understand the profession

Learn how coding differs from billing, what coders review, and the kinds of healthcare settings where coding knowledge is used.

2

Build your foundation

Medical terminology, anatomy, coding guidelines, documentation, and code-book navigation all contribute to strong coding skills.

3

Learn the major code sets

Professional coding education commonly includes ICD-10-CM, CPT®, and HCPCS Level II, along with the rules for applying them correctly.

4

Practice applying guidelines

Coding is more than locating a code. Students need practice interpreting documentation, following official guidelines, and selecting the best-supported answer.

5

Prepare for certification

Once foundational training is in place, certification preparation helps students strengthen speed, code-book navigation, scenario work, and exam strategy.

6

Plan your next step

Your next step may be structured training, self-paced education, certification study, or additional foundational work depending on where you are starting.

Important: A one-hour webinar cannot replace comprehensive medical coding education. Its purpose is to help you understand the career path, avoid common beginner confusion, and make a more informed decision about what to do next.
