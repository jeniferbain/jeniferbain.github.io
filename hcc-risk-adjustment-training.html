Independent Auditing & Quality Assurance

HCC coding audit support for existing teams

Already have HCC coders or internal auditors? MediCode Bootcamp offers supplemental, independent reviews to help organizations evaluate documentation supportability, coding accuracy, and the consistency of audit findings.

Coder Accuracy Audits

Review coded records against applicable guidelines and provider documentation, identify supported and unsupported diagnoses, and summarize quality findings.

Second-Level Audit Reviews

Independently assess audit determinations, including whether identified coding errors and audit findings are justified by the medical record.

Provider Documentation Reviews

Evaluate chronic-condition documentation and code supportability, with findings that can inform documentation education and quality improvement.

Available for defined projects, supplemental audit capacity, and audit-informed coder education. Scope, review standards, record access, confidentiality, and reporting are established with each organization before work begins. These are independent quality services, not official CMS RADV audits.
