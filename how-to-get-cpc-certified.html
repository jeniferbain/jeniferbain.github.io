How to Get CPC Certified: A Step-by-Step Guide

If you are considering a career in medical coding, the CPC® credential is one path into professional physician-office coding. This guide explains the major steps—from learning the fundamentals through taking the AAPC CPC exam and understanding apprentice status afterward.

What does CPC certification demonstrate?

The Certified Professional Coder (CPC) exam evaluates professional medical coding knowledge, including application of CPT®, HCPCS Level II, and ICD-10-CM codes, modifiers, coding guidelines, and regulatory concepts. Preparation also requires a working foundation in anatomy, physiology, and medical terminology.

You do not have to already be an experienced coder to begin learning. The important first step is building enough coding knowledge and practice to prepare responsibly for the certification exam.

The CPC roadmap

Steps to becoming CPC certified

1

Build your medical coding foundation

Learn medical terminology, anatomy, coding guidelines, ICD-10-CM diagnosis coding, CPT® procedure coding, HCPCS Level II, modifiers, and code-book navigation. A structured training program can help organize these subjects and provide practice applying them.

MediCode Bootcamp offers both live online CPC classes and a self-paced CPC course with weekly instructor support.

2

Prepare specifically for the CPC exam

After learning the material, shift part of your preparation toward exam-style practice. Work on locating information efficiently, applying guidelines, answering coding questions accurately, and managing your time.

If you have already completed coding education and only need focused exam preparation, a CPC exam review may be more appropriate than repeating a full training course.

3

Maintain AAPC membership and purchase your exam

AAPC requires current membership to sit for a certification exam. You will also need an exam voucher and must meet AAPC's current registration and identification requirements. Because exam policies and pricing can change, verify the latest requirements directly with AAPC before scheduling.

4

Take and pass the CPC exam

The current AAPC CPC exam contains 100 multiple-choice questions, allows 4 hours, and requires an overall score of 70% or higher to pass. AAPC offers testing-center and at-home proctored testing options.

5

Understand CPC-A apprentice status

If you pass the CPC exam without meeting AAPC's experience requirement for full CPC standing, your credential generally carries the CPC-A apprentice designation. The “A” indicates that you passed the certification exam and are building the experience needed for full CPC status.

AAPC provides multiple ways to satisfy apprentice-removal requirements through qualifying work experience and approved combinations of education and practical experience. Always use AAPC's current apprentice-removal guidance when you are ready to submit documentation.

6

Keep developing real-world coding skills

Passing an exam is an important milestone, but professional coding also requires continued practice with provider documentation, coding guidelines, payer requirements, accuracy, compliance, and workflow. Continue building practical experience and professional knowledge after certification.

Frequently asked questions

CPC Certification FAQ

Do I need experience before taking the CPC exam?

AAPC's exam registration requirements do not list prior coding work experience as a prerequisite for the CPC exam. New coders who pass without meeting the experience requirement generally receive CPC-A apprentice status.

What score do I need to pass?

AAPC currently requires an overall score of 70% or higher, meaning at least 70 correct answers out of 100 questions.

Can I take the CPC exam from home?

AAPC currently offers an at-home proctored exam as well as testing-center exams. Check AAPC's current technical and testing requirements before scheduling.

What coding books should I learn to use?

CPC-focused training uses ICD-10-CM, CPT® Professional, and HCPCS Level II. Students should become comfortable navigating these code sets and applying their guidelines.

What is the difference between CPC and CPC-A?

Both indicate that the certification exam has been passed. CPC-A includes the apprentice designation for a coder who has not yet fulfilled AAPC's experience requirement for full CPC standing.
