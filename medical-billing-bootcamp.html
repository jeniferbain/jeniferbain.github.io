Is This Medical Billing Boot Camp for You?

This live Medical Billing Boot Camp is designed for beginners who want practical, instructor-led training and a better understanding of how medical billing works in the real world.

This boot camp may be a great fit if you are:

  • Completely new to medical billing
  • Considering medical billing as a new career
  • Currently learning medical billing and want additional hands-on practice
  • Working in healthcare and want to better understand the billing process
  • A new medical biller who wants to build confidence with claims and reimbursement
  • Interested in learning the revenue cycle from beginning to end

No prior medical billing experience is required. We'll start with the foundations and build from there.

What You'll Learn

During this two-day live boot camp, you'll build a practical foundation in the medical billing process and learn how claims move through the revenue cycle from start to finish.

  • How the medical billing revenue cycle works
  • Patient registration, insurance information, and eligibility basics
  • Commercial insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, and patient responsibility
  • How ICD-10-CM, CPT, and HCPCS codes relate to medical billing
  • How to understand and work with the CMS-1500 claim form
  • Claim submission and clearinghouse basics
  • The difference between rejected and denied claims
  • How to read EOBs and ERAs
  • Payment posting and adjustments
  • Denials, corrected claims, appeals, and claim follow-up
  • Accounts receivable and patient balances
  • Basic billing compliance and HIPAA considerations
  • Practical billing scenarios and real-world problem solving
