How Much Does CPC Certification Cost?

The total cost of becoming CPC certified is more than just the exam fee. Your budget may include medical coding training, AAPC membership, a CPC exam voucher, coding books, and optional exam-preparation resources. Here is a simple way to understand the major expenses.

Frequently asked questions

CPC Certification Cost FAQ

How much does the CPC exam cost in 2026?

AAPC currently publishes core certification exam pricing of $425 for one attempt or $499 for two attempts. Check AAPC before purchase because pricing and promotions can change.

Is AAPC membership included in the CPC exam fee?

Do not assume it is included. Current AAPC membership is required to sit for an AAPC certification exam, so account for membership separately unless the specific package you purchase says otherwise.

Do I have to buy coding books?

You need access to the appropriate code sets for CPC preparation and testing. MediCode Bootcamp students purchase their own coding books. AAPC recommends current-year books, although its exam policy currently permits the current or immediately preceding year's books.

Does MediCode Bootcamp's $597 live CPC Boot Camp include the CPC exam?

No. The CPC certification exam fee and required coding books are separate from the live Boot Camp tuition.

Which MediCode Bootcamp CPC option costs less?

The current live CPC Boot Camp is $597. The self-paced program is $1,297 and provides 12 months of course access plus weekly instructor sessions and additional included resources. The right choice depends on whether you prioritize a lower tuition and accelerated schedule or longer-term flexibility and support.
