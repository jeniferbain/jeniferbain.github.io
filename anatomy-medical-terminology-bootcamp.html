What to expect

A focused weekend foundation

This two-day live boot camp is designed to strengthen the anatomy and medical terminology knowledge used throughout medical coding and billing without turning the weekend into weeks of coursework.

  • Major body systems and essential anatomy
  • Medical word building, roots, prefixes, and suffixes
  • Clinical terminology used in diagnoses and procedures
  • Connections to medical coding and billing
  • Live instruction with opportunities to ask questions
