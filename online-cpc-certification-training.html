What CPC students learn

Build the foundation used in professional medical coding

CPC preparation involves more than memorizing codes. Students need a working understanding of medical terminology, anatomy, coding guidelines, ICD-10-CM diagnosis coding, CPT® procedure coding, HCPCS Level II, modifiers, documentation, and practical code-book navigation.

Already completed a coding course and only need final exam preparation?

If you have already completed coding education and are preparing to sit for the CPC exam, a CPC exam review may be a better fit than enrolling in a full CPC training program. Call or text Jenifer if you are unsure which level of training you need.

Frequently asked questions

Online CPC training FAQ

Can I take CPC training completely online?

Yes. MediCode Bootcamp's CPC options are online. The live Boot Camp uses scheduled real-time classes, while the self-paced option lets you work more flexibly and includes weekly instructor sessions.

Which CPC training option is best for a beginner?

That depends on your learning style. The live CPC Boot Camp is beginner-friendly and provides a highly structured schedule. The self-paced program is better suited to students who need more flexibility and want a longer period of instructor support.

Do I need to buy coding books?

Yes. Students purchase their own current coding books. For CPC-focused training, that includes ICD-10-CM, CPT® Professional, and HCPCS Level II.

Is the CPC certification exam included in the live Boot Camp?

No. The CPC certification exam fee is separate from the live CPC Boot Camp tuition.

Can I talk to someone before I choose a CPC course?

Yes. Call or text Jenifer at (574) 213-8793. You can discuss your background, schedule, learning preferences, and which CPC training format may fit you better.
